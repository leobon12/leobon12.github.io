01

The 3/8 Exponent for Shortest Directed Paths

In a KMSW cell encoded by n independent uniform steps from {(1, −1), (−1, 0), (0, 1)}, let Δn be the largest finite shortest directed-path distance, measured in edges. Every median mn of Δn satisfies mn = n3/8+o(1).

02

Graph Limits of Longest Directed Paths

For the uniform infinite bipolar-oriented triangulation, rescale north- and west-going exploration times by n and longest directed-path lengths by n3/4. The resulting fields are tight, and every subsequential closed-graph limit is almost surely a locally bounded continuous function on the limiting domain of directed-comparable edge pairs.

03

Nontrivial Limits of Shortest Directed Paths

For the uniform infinite bipolar-oriented triangulation, rescale exploration times by n and shortest directed-path lengths by the upper integer median of the maximal finite distance in an n-step KMSW cell. Subsequential closed-graph limits are continuous functions. In limits taken jointly with the primal and dual peanosphere data, the function is almost surely not identically zero.

04

Mesh-Only Convergence for Orthogonal Tilings

For orthogonal tilings in dimension d ≥ 2 with mesh ε → 0, discrete harmonic extensions of smooth harmonic boundary data converge uniformly without the original theorem’s additional geometric alternatives. For h harmonic and C2 near a bounded domain compactly inside the tiled region, the supplement gives error ≤ C‖h‖C² ε4/(d+4), hence exponent 2/3 in the plane. Under the remaining domain hypotheses, harmonic measures on balls and stopped walks also converge, with walks considered modulo time parametrization.

05

Sphere Packings and Tangency-Polytope Tilings

For every nonempty open Ω ⊂ ℝd, d ∈ {2, 3, 4}, there is a packing by arbitrarily small balls whose tangency polytopes form a locally finite, face-to-face tiling of exactly Ω. Ball tangencies correspond precisely to shared facets. Each tile has at most 6, 12, or 24 facets in dimensions 2, 3, or 4, respectively.

Sphere Averages in Odd Dimensions

For odd d = 2m + 1 ≥ 3, the spherical-average process St = he⁻ᵗ(0) − h1(0) of the whole-space log-correlated Gaussian field has an explicit Brownian convolution representation. Its paths are locally Cm−1,α for every 0 < α < 1, but it has no mth mean-square derivative.

07

Quantum Cones in Odd Dimensions

For odd d ≥ 3, 0 < γ < √(2d), and β < Q = d/γ + γ/2, recentering the spherical-average process at its first crossing of −b after subtracting (Q − β)t gives a total-variation limit on each fixed radial tail as b → ∞. The associated rescaled fields converge locally in total variation to a β-quantum cone.

08

Reflected Random Walks with Spatial Singularities

Under the stated connectivity, mass-transport, ergodicity, and finite-moment assumptions on a planar cell environment, the interpolated reflected walk from a fixed starting cell, with area-over-conductance holding times, converges diffusively for almost every environment to Brownian motion with deterministic positive-definite covariance. The main note permits a closed singular set of zero one-dimensional Hausdorff measure and requires spatial local finiteness off that set. The companion treats whole-plane coverings with connectivity along almost every horizontal and vertical line, without that local-finiteness requirement.

Gaussian Fluctuations of LQG Distance

Proposed theorem: for a whole-plane GFF normalized by h1(0) = 0, normalize the metric so the median global left–right distance across the unit square is one. Then (Dhγ(0, 1) − 1)/γ converges in law to N(−q/2, 3/8), where q < 0 is the median of the minimum, over y ∈ [0, 1], of ∫01 h(x + iy) dx. The line integrals are defined by L2 limits.

Two-Thirds Geodesic Deviation

Let Mγ be the maximum Euclidean distance from [0, 1] reached by the whole-plane LQG geodesic from 0 to 1. Then Mγ/γ2/3 is tight and bounded away from zero in probability as γ ↓ 0. Quantitatively, 𝔼Mγ ≤ Cγ2/3 and ℙ[Mγ ≤ aγ2/3] ≤ Ca3/5 for 0 < a ≤ 1 and sufficiently small γ.
