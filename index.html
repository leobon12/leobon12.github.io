I am a PhD student in mathematics at the University of Chicago, advised by Ewain Gwynne. Previously, I was an undergraduate at Stanford University.

Research

I am interested in probability and random geometry, particularly Liouville quantum gravity, random planar maps, and random walks in random environments.

Teaching

I have taught calculus and mentored undergraduate research at the University of Chicago.

I also gave the complex analysis lecture for WOMP 2025, the mathematics department’s orientation program for incoming graduate students.
